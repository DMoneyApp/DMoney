Your personal finances, organised.

DMoney is an offline-first personal finance tracker designed to help you record and review income, expenses, accounts, budgets, financial goals and recurring commitments in one place.

Designed for personal recordkeeping

  • Record income, expenses and transfers.
  • Organise accounts and review spending summaries.
  • Track financial goals and recurring commitments.
  • Use local storage and continue working offline where supported.
  • Optionally export a backup or connect Google Drive for user-initiated backup and restore.

How backup works

DMoney is not a bank and does not connect to your bank accounts to fetch transactions. You enter your own records. Local records stay in the relevant device/browser or Android app storage. If you choose Google Drive backup, DMoney uses Google authorization to create or restore backup files in your Drive. Keep an independent backup and verify that it completed successfully.
