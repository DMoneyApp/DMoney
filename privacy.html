DMoney Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DMoney, a personal finance tracking application, handles information when you use the DMoney website or Android application. DMoney is designed to be offline-first. Please read this policy before entering financial information or enabling backup features.

1. Information you enter

DMoney may store the information you choose to enter, such as transactions, income and expenses, account names and balances, budgets, goals, recurring commitments and notes. This information may be financial or personal. Do not enter passwords, full payment-card details, bank login credentials or other secrets into notes or transaction fields.

2. Where your data is stored

DMoney's offline-first website stores app data in browser storage on the device where you use it. The Android app also uses app-private local storage for its local data and recovery snapshot. These local records are not automatically available on your other devices. Clearing browser/app data, uninstalling the app, device loss, corruption or a software problem may cause loss of data. Keep a separate backup.

DMoney does not provide a bank account, payment service, financial institution account or a developer-operated cloud database for your transaction records. This statement describes the current app design; if future versions add online accounts, analytics, advertising or other data collection, this policy must be updated before those features are used.

3. Optional backup and Google user data

If you choose to connect Google Drive, DMoney requests Google's authorization to access the Drive files it creates or that the user opens with the app (the drive.file scope). Depending on the sign-in flow, DMoney may also receive basic Google account information such as your email address to identify the connected account in the app.

  • Google Drive access is used to let you create, list and restore DMoney backup files.
  • Backup files can contain the DMoney data you chose to save, including financial records. They are stored in your Google Drive account, in the DMoney Backups area used by the app.
  • Backup/restore is user-initiated. DMoney does not need your Google password and does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it for unrelated purposes.
  • Google Drive is operated by Google and is subject to Google's own terms and privacy practices. Your Drive account settings and anyone you share backup files with may affect who can access those files.
  • You can revoke DMoney's Google access in your Google Account security settings and can delete backup files from Google Drive. Revoking access prevents future Drive actions until you authorize again; it does not automatically delete existing Drive backups.

DMoney's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing backup and restore feature, for security, or as required by law.

4. Sharing and sale

DMoney does not sell your transaction records or use Google Drive data for advertising. The app sends data to Google Drive only when you choose to use the Drive backup/restore feature. If you export or share a backup yourself, the recipient and any service you choose may access its contents. We may disclose information if legally required, but DMoney does not operate a developer-side transaction database to routinely receive your local records.

5. Security and retention

We aim to minimise collection and use the storage mechanisms described above, but no device, app, cloud service or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device and Google Account with a strong screen lock and account security. Do not share backup files publicly. Local data remains until it is deleted from the relevant browser/app storage or the app is removed; Drive backups remain until you delete them or they are otherwise removed under Google's policies. The app may retain only a limited number of recent backups according to its current backup settings.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to connect Google Drive, export a local backup, delete records within DMoney, clear the app's local data, or delete DMoney backup files from your Drive. Before clearing data or uninstalling, export a backup and confirm that the file is safely stored. For privacy questions or requests concerning information handled by DMoney, contact support.dmoney@gmail.com. Because most financial records are stored on your device or in your own Drive account, we cannot remotely retrieve or delete records we do not hold.

7. Children

DMoney is a general personal-finance tool and is not designed specifically for children. Do not use it to collect another person's information without appropriate authority.

8. International use and legal rights

Your device and Google Drive account may store or process information in locations selected by you or by Google. Privacy rights and legal requirements depend on your circumstances and applicable law, including any applicable UAE personal-data protection requirements. Nothing in this policy is intended to remove rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when DMoney's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision. Please review this page periodically and before enabling new features.

10. Contact

Privacy and support contact: support.dmoney@gmail.com

This document is a plain-language product privacy notice, not a legal opinion or a guarantee of compliance in every jurisdiction. The actual app behaviour and all in-app disclosures must remain consistent with this policy.
